| **1.6.0** | **New:**<br>• Pinged tamed animals show their mood next to their name, like Boar (Hungry) or Husk (Happy), and animals being tamed show their progress, like Wolf (15%).<br>• Pinged fish show their quality, like Pufferfish • 3, and have their own toggle and color (**Enable Fish**, **Ping Fish Color**).<br>• Ping a fish before it bites and it stays outlined until you land it or it gets away.<br>• A Fishsight slot beside the Guardian Power shows while a fish is hooked: **Left Alt + Z** outlines that fish or turns it off. Turn on **Auto Outline Hooked Fish** to outline every catch.<br>• **Ping Duration** sets how long pings stay (vanilla 5 s).<br>• In Settings > Controls, PingMark's keys now show the mod's name in grey, like **PingMark · Ping**.<br>**Changes:**<br>• Perception only senses what you can see: buried treasure and things behind walls or rock are left out (turn off **Perception Line Of Sight** for the old behavior).<br>• Perception has a new painted icon, on its status and its slot.<br>• The Perception slot lines up evenly with other mods' ability slots beside the Guardian Power, such as TempestRite's Rally Call, and the slots sit closer together: their names are narrower and shrink to fit. Turn off **Show Ability Names** to hide the names and bring the slots closer still.<br>• The Useful list no longer holds crafting station upgrades, statues, waymarkers or other decorations you can't use; crafting stations themselves stay, and statues and waymarkers are now Locations.<br>• Valuables no longer holds build pieces, or their copies in villages, that only give materials when broken, such as cauldrons; chests, pots, beehives, sap collectors and trophy stands stay, and village braziers and bonfires are now Useful.<br>Press **Auto Sort** in the collections editor to update a list you already have. |
| **1.5.0** | **New:**<br>• A Perception slot next to your Guardian Power shows when Perception is ready, with a countdown after use; the Perception Fatigue status icon is gone.<br>• Ping and Perception keys can be changed in the game's Settings > Controls.<br>• Perception now senses everything Ping Targets allows, such as gravestones, other players and, with Enable All Other, any object.<br>**Changes:**<br>• Perception lasts 15 s and is ready again 60 s after use, like a Guardian Power (new Fixed default; Relative mode is still available).<br>**Important:**<br>• Update together with your server.<br>• Existing configs keep their old Perception timings; set **Perception Duration** 15, **Perception Fatigue Mode** Fixed and **Perception Fatigue Duration** 60 for the new ones. |
| **1.4.0** | **New:**<br>• Perception becomes an ability with its own status, a colored burst and chime, and Perception Fatigue afterwards (Relative mode grows it with what you sensed).<br>• Perception ends early once everything found is looted or slain.<br>• Cut logs can be pinged.<br>**Auto Sort:**<br>• Traders and their outposts are always sorted.<br>• Wood-only objects are no longer valuables.<br>**Changes:**<br>• Z pings while moving; Perception only works standing still.<br>**Fixes:**<br>• Broken ore deposits keep their name and outline. |
| **1.3.0** | **New:**<br>• Perception (**Left Shift + Z**) outlines listed objects and drops around you, and can highlight nearby creatures.<br>• Birds can be pinged.<br>• The Prefab Collections menu can copy and import YAML and export a sorting report.<br>**Auto Sort:**<br>• Smarter with modded prefabs.<br>• Rocks, bushes and debris are left out.<br>**Fixes:**<br>• Dungeon walls and location geometry are no longer outlined. |
| **1.2.0** | **New:**<br>• Pings from others show names in your language and outline the exact object.<br>• Undo for removed sections.<br>• Tidier Configuration Manager settings.<br>**Servers:**<br>• Only admins can change collections, now kept in their own file.<br>• Ping spam is limited.<br>**Fixes:**<br>• Many stability, nameplate and shortcut fixes, plus better performance. |
