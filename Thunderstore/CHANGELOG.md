| Version | Update Notes |
|----------|---------------|
| **1.5.0** | **New:** A Perception slot next to your Guardian Power shows when Perception is ready, with a countdown after use; the Perception Fatigue status icon is gone. Ping and Perception keys can be changed in the game's Settings > Controls. Perception now senses everything Ping Targets allows, such as gravestones, other players and, with Enable All Other, any object.<br>**Changes:** Perception lasts 15 s and is ready again 60 s after use, like a Guardian Power (new Fixed default; Relative mode is still available).<br>**Important:** Update together with your server. Existing configs keep their old Perception timings; set Perception Duration 15, Perception Fatigue Mode Fixed and Perception Fatigue Duration 60 for the new ones. |
| **1.4.0** | **New:** Perception becomes an ability with its own status, a colored burst and chime, and Perception Fatigue afterwards (Relative mode grows it with what you sensed). It ends early once everything found is looted or slain. Cut logs can be pinged.<br>**Auto Sort:** Traders and their outposts are always sorted; wood-only objects are no longer valuables.<br>**Changes:** Z pings while moving; Perception only works standing still.<br>**Fixes:** Broken ore deposits keep their name and outline. |
| **1.3.0** | **New:** Perception (Left Shift + Z) outlines listed objects and drops around you, and can highlight nearby creatures. Birds can be pinged. The Prefab Collections menu can copy and import YAML and export a sorting report.<br>**Auto Sort:** Smarter with modded prefabs; rocks, bushes and debris are left out.<br>**Fixes:** Dungeon walls and location geometry are no longer outlined. |
| **1.2.0** | **New:** Pings from others show names in your language and outline the exact object. Undo for removed sections, tidier Configuration Manager settings.<br>**Servers:** Only admins can change collections, now kept in their own file. Ping spam is limited.<br>**Fixes:** Many stability, nameplate and shortcut fixes, plus better performance. |
